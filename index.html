*The Newport County Dinner Club is a two-for-one dining program. An entrée is defined as the main course of a meal as identified on the participating restaurant's standard menu. When two entrées of different prices are ordered, the least expensive qualifying entrée will be deducted. Appetizers and desserts are not considered entrées. Participating restaurants may establish individual valid dates, times, restrictions and blackout dates.
