Common questions

Do I still need my printed book?

Yes. This season the discount is redeemed with the printed book. The app is your guide to the restaurants.

How does the two-for-one work?

Order two qualifying entrées, and the lower-priced one comes off the bill. Appetizers and desserts are not entrées. Dine-in only: not valid on takeout, delivery or online orders.

Why can't I use it on a certain day?

Each restaurant sets its own valid dates, times and blackout days. You'll find them on that restaurant's page in the app and in your book. When in doubt, ask your server before ordering.

Where can I buy a book?

Online through the app's "Buy a book" button, or at participating local retailers listed on the app's "The Club" tab.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in, and the app doesn't collect your personal information. See the privacy policy.

I own a restaurant. How do I join?

Joining is by invitation. Use the request form on the app's "The Club" tab, or email us.
