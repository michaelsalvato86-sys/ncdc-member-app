Privacy Policy

Newport County Dinner Club app · Effective [EFFECTIVE DATE]

The short version: the app doesn't ask for your name, email or any account. It doesn't track you, show ads or sell anything about you. If you tap "Near me", your location is used on your phone only, to sort restaurants by distance, and is never sent to us.

Who we are

The Newport County Dinner Club app is published by [NEWPORT LIFE LEGAL ENTITY NAME] ("we", "us"), which operates the Newport County Dinner Club. This policy covers the Dinner Club app on iPhone and Android and its web version.

What the app collects

We don't collect personal information through the app. There is no sign-in, no account and no form to fill in. We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and the app doesn't set cookies.

Your location

The "Near me" button asks your phone for its location so the app can list the closest restaurants first. That calculation happens on your phone. Your location isn't sent to us or stored by the app, and you can say no and still use everything else. You can change this any time in your phone's settings.

Services the app relies on

To show maps, fonts and some app code, your phone loads content from these providers. Like any website, they receive basic technical information such as your IP address and device type when your phone requests that content:

  • OpenStreetMap, for map images (privacy policy)
  • Google Fonts, for the typefaces (privacy policy)
  • Cloudflare (cdnjs), which hosts open-source code the app uses (privacy policy)

We don't receive or store any of this information.

Links to other sites

The app links to restaurant websites, booking services, map apps and the club's online store. When you follow a link, you leave the app, and that site's own privacy policy applies. Book purchases are handled by Square. We never see your card details.

Children

The app is a general-audience restaurant guide. It isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version of the app adds features that use personal information, such as a digital membership card, we will update this policy before those features go live and change the date at the top.
